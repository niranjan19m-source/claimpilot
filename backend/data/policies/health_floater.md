# Family Floater Health Insurance — Policy Wording (Synthetic, for demonstration)

Product code: HEALTH-FLT. This wording is a synthetic document modelled on the structure of Indian indemnity health policies. It is not the wording of any real insurer.

### H-1.1 Period of Insurance and Premium
Cover applies to hospitalisation that begins during the policy period, provided the premium has been received in full. A policy in grace period does not cover hospitalisation that begins during the grace period.

### H-1.2 Insured Persons
Only the members named in the Schedule are insured. The sum insured is shared on a floater basis among all insured members.

### H-2.1 In-patient Hospitalisation
The Company will indemnify reasonable and customary medical expenses for in-patient hospitalisation of at least 24 consecutive hours, when advised by a registered medical practitioner, up to the sum insured.

### H-2.2 Day Care Procedures
Listed day care procedures requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalisation due to technological advancement are covered.

### H-2.3 Pre- and Post-Hospitalisation
Medical expenses incurred up to 30 days before and 60 days after hospitalisation are covered, provided the in-patient claim is admissible.

### H-3.1 Room Rent Limit
Room rent, boarding and nursing are payable up to 1% of the sum insured per day for a normal room and 2% for ICU. If a higher category room is occupied, all associated medical expenses (except medicines, consumables and implants) are paid in the proportion that the eligible room rent bears to the actual room rent.

### H-3.2 Co-payment
A co-payment of 10% applies to every admissible claim for an insured member aged 61 years or above at the time of admission.

### H-3.3 Cataract Sub-limit
Cataract treatment is payable up to INR 40,000 per eye per policy year.

### H-4.1 Initial Waiting Period
Expenses for any illness within the first 30 days from the first policy commencement date are excluded, except claims arising from an accident.

### H-4.2 Pre-existing Diseases
Expenses related to the treatment of a pre-existing disease (PED) and its direct complications are excluded until the expiry of 36 months of continuous coverage after the date of inception of the first policy. A PED is any condition diagnosed, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within 48 months before the first policy was issued.

### H-4.3 Specified Disease Waiting Period
The following are excluded until the expiry of 24 months of continuous coverage: cataract, benign prostatic hypertrophy, hernia, hydrocele, joint replacement surgery, kidney stones, gallbladder stones, sinusitis, and varicose veins.

### H-5.1 Exclusion — Cosmetic and Obesity Treatment
Cosmetic or plastic surgery, weight control and obesity treatment are excluded unless required as part of reconstruction following an accident or cancer.

### H-5.2 Exclusion — Self-inflicted Injury and Substance Abuse
Intentional self-injury and treatment for alcohol or drug abuse, and any injury sustained under the influence of alcohol or drugs, are excluded.

### H-5.3 Exclusion — Investigation and Evaluation
Admission primarily for diagnostic, X-ray or laboratory investigations not followed by active treatment is excluded.

### H-6.1 Condition — Intimation
Planned hospitalisation must be intimated 48 hours before admission and emergency hospitalisation within 24 hours of admission.

### H-6.2 Condition — Documents Required
Claims shall be supported by: the claim form, discharge summary, final hospital bill with itemised break-up, payment receipts, investigation reports, and prescriptions.

### H-6.3 Condition — Network and Reimbursement
Cashless facility is available only at network hospitals. Claims at non-network hospitals are settled by reimbursement. The Company may decline treatment at hospitals placed on its exclusion list for prior irregularities.

### H-6.4 Condition — Fraud
If any claim is fraudulent, or supported by a fraudulent document or declaration, all benefits under the policy are forfeited and premium paid is not refunded.
