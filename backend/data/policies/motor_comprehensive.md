# Private Car Package Policy — Policy Wording (Synthetic, for demonstration)

Product code: MOTOR-PKG. This wording is a synthetic document modelled on the structure of Indian private-car package policies. It is not the wording of any real insurer.

### M-1.1 Period of Insurance
Cover operates only for loss or damage occurring between 00:00 hours on the policy start date and midnight on the policy end date shown in the Schedule. Loss occurring outside the period of insurance is not covered.

### M-1.2 Premium Payment Condition
No liability attaches under this policy unless the premium has been paid in full and realised. If the premium cheque or mandate is dishonoured, the policy is void from inception.

### M-1.3 Insured Vehicle
Cover applies only to the vehicle whose registration number, engine number and chassis number are stated in the Schedule. Loss to any other vehicle is not covered under Section I.

### M-2.1 Section I — Own Damage: Accidental External Means
The Company will indemnify the insured against loss or damage to the insured vehicle and its accessories caused by accidental external means, including collision, overturning, and impact with any object.

### M-2.2 Section I — Fire, Explosion, Self-Ignition and Lightning
Loss or damage caused by fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning is covered.

### M-2.3 Section I — Burglary, Housebreaking and Theft
Loss or damage by burglary, housebreaking or theft is covered, subject to the theft conditions in M-6.3. A First Information Report (FIR) must be lodged with the police and a copy provided to the Company.

### M-2.4 Section I — Natural Calamities
Loss or damage caused by flood, inundation, storm, cyclone, hurricane, hailstorm, earthquake or landslide is covered. Claims under this clause are verified against meteorological records for the location and date of loss.

### M-2.5 Section I — Malicious Act, Riot and Strike
Loss or damage caused by riot, strike, malicious act or terrorist activity is covered.

### M-2.6 Section I — Glass Breakage
Damage to the windshield and windows is covered. Glass is not subject to depreciation. A windshield-only claim does not affect the No Claim Bonus where the add-on is opted.

### M-3.1 Compulsory Deductible
Each and every claim under Section I is subject to a compulsory deductible of INR 1,000 for vehicles up to 1500cc and INR 2,000 above 1500cc, in addition to any voluntary deductible stated in the Schedule.

### M-3.2 Depreciation on Parts Replaced
In the case of partial loss, depreciation is deducted from the cost of parts replaced: 50% on rubber, nylon, plastic parts, tyres, tubes and batteries; 30% on fibreglass components; nil on glass. Metal parts depreciate by vehicle age: not exceeding 6 months nil; 6 months to 1 year 5%; 1 to 2 years 10%; 2 to 3 years 15%; 3 to 4 years 25%; 4 to 5 years 35%; 5 to 10 years 40%; over 10 years 50%.

### M-3.3 Insured Declared Value (IDV)
The maximum liability of the Company under Section I for any one claim shall not exceed the Insured Declared Value of the vehicle stated in the Schedule.

### M-3.4 Total Loss and Constructive Total Loss
Where the cost of repair exceeds 75% of the IDV, the vehicle is treated as a Constructive Total Loss and the claim is settled on a total-loss basis at IDV less salvage, subject to the deductible.

### M-4.1 Exclusion — Wear, Tear and Mechanical Breakdown
The Company is not liable for consequential loss, depreciation, wear and tear, or mechanical or electrical breakdown, failure or breakage.

### M-4.2 Exclusion — Driving Under the Influence
The Company is not liable for any accidental loss or damage suffered whilst the insured or any person driving with the knowledge and consent of the insured is under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs.

### M-4.3 Exclusion — Driver Without a Valid Licence
The Company is not liable for loss or damage occurring while the vehicle is driven by any person who does not hold an effective and valid driving licence.

### M-4.4 Exclusion — Use for Hire or Reward
Cover does not apply where the private car is used for hire or reward, carriage of goods, organised racing or speed testing, contrary to the Limitations as to Use.

### M-4.5 Exclusion — Consequential Engine Damage from Water
Damage to the engine caused by ingress of water (hydrostatic lock) resulting from the vehicle being driven or cranked in water-logged conditions is not covered, unless the Engine Protect add-on is opted.

### M-5.1 Add-on — Zero Depreciation
Where Zero Depreciation is opted and shown in the Schedule, depreciation under M-3.2 is not deducted for up to two claims in the policy year.

### M-5.2 Add-on — Engine Protect
Where Engine Protect is opted, the exclusion in M-4.5 does not apply and consequential engine damage due to water ingress is covered.

### M-6.1 Condition — Notice of Loss
Notice shall be given to the Company immediately, and in any case within 48 hours, of any accidental loss or damage. Delayed notice may prejudice the Company's ability to investigate and may result in the claim being declined where the delay is unexplained.

### M-6.2 Condition — Documents Required
The insured shall provide: a completed claim form, a copy of the Registration Certificate, a copy of the driving licence of the person driving at the time of loss, photographs of the damage, and a repair estimate from the repairer.

### M-6.3 Condition — Theft
For theft claims the insured shall additionally provide the FIR, all original keys, and, where the vehicle is not traced within 90 days, the police non-traceable report.

### M-6.4 Condition — Fraud
If any claim is in any respect fraudulent, or if any fraudulent means or devices are used by the insured or anyone acting on the insured's behalf to obtain any benefit, all benefits under this policy shall be forfeited.

### M-7.1 Section II — Third Party Liability
Third-party bodily injury and property damage are covered under Section II. Third-party claims involving bodily injury or death are referred to the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal process and must be handled by a senior adjuster.
